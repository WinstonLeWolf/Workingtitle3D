# Accessibility

Workingtitle3D is built by someone with a rare form of color blindness, for everyone who sees
color differently or not at all.

## What it does now

- No state is shown by hue alone. Selected, hole, locked, open and checked are said in
  words, marks or lightness as well.
- Errors use one bright yellow with the word ERROR, readable in grayscale.
- Three color schemes in the View menu: Green (default), Blue (for red-green color
  blindness) and High contrast (white and grays only).
- Text contrast on the default scheme measures 5.7:1 to 15:1 against the background.
- Tooltips in the View menu explain each control on hover, and say why a greyed-out one is
  unavailable. They are off by default.
- Every action has a menu item, and most have a key.

## Offline and account-free

It works with no network at all and asks for no account. Many free modellers demand a login
even when they run on your own machine. This one does not, and will not. Being able to use a
tool without handing over an identity is part of being able to use it at all.

## Known gaps

- The 3D view is not usable with a screen reader.
- Part colors you pick yourself use a standard palette and do not change with the scheme.
- Keyboard focus is not yet shown clearly on every control.

## Report a problem

Open an issue describing what you could not do and what you use (screen reader, color
vision type, keyboard only). Accessibility problems are treated as bugs.
